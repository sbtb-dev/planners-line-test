# The boundary register

The register lists boundaries that state a route under the [line test](../line-test/RULE.md). The list is in [BOUNDARIES.md](BOUNDARIES.md).

## What a row means

A row means that a published boundary states a route (currency or record) and meets the three tests on paper. The three tests are in reading note 4: the boundary sorts items before the buy, the seller does not decide membership, and a buy can fail.

A row is not an endorsement.

"Shown in practice" needs evidence that the register can cite. Until then, the status stays "On paper."

## How to propose a row

Anyone can propose a row. Use the issue template "Propose a boundary." The same terms apply to every proposal, whoever makes it. The proposal must give:

- the published text of the boundary, and where it is published;
- the route it states;
- how it meets each of the three tests;
- who stewards it.

A seller may propose a boundary about its own product. The row is written in this repository's words. Seller claims are marked as claims.
