# Fixture 01: run through the prompt, v0.4.0

Route: prompt/PROMPT.md. A fresh subagent. Input given:

> A retailer's gaming hub. We sell onsite placements on the hub, creator content, social amplification, sweepstakes, in-store events, and targeting on gamer profiles. One package price. Gamers pay high attention on our hub.

## Answer

Input type: an offer. I use the offer form. Every fact below is the seller's claim.

**1. What it is**

| # | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Onsite placements on the hub |
| 2 | Creator content |
| 3 | Social amplification |
| 4 | Sweepstakes |
| 5 | In-store events |
| 6 | Targeting on gamer profiles |

**2. Where each part goes**

| # | Line | Compare with | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retail media | That retailer's other onsite units, and other retailers' onsite units | M-10 |
| 2 | Creator | The creator line's units | M-07, SR-performs-message |
| 3 | Social | The social line's units | M-12 |
| 4 | Promotions | Cost per entry or redemption | M-16 |
| 5 | Experiential, by parts | Each part separately | M-17 |
| 6 | Not a unit. It is targeting on the line that carries it. | That line's other segments, on the same units | M-24, SR-targeting |

Each payment goes to its own line (S-6.1, SR-payments). The deal name "package" does not decide.

The claim "Gamers pay high attention on our hub" is a property of the delivery. Attention does not change the unit (N-2). It is compared inside the line of part 1 (M-10). The offer does not state that the price is counted on attention. So N-9 does not apply now. See part 4.

**3. What has no native line**

Nothing in this offer is a brand's presence inside play. Route stated: not applicable. No comparison is stated. The attention claim has no comparison with it. Label any engagement count as media engagement (C-3.2).

**4. What to confirm**

- The offer sets one package price. Ask for the price of each of the six parts (S-6.1).
- Part 1: the unit, the price basis, and the currency. Are they impressions, viewable impressions, or something else? Who counts them?
- Part 1: does the seller count the price on attention or time? If yes, the format's price basis differs. The unit is then unplaced (N-9). Ask if the seller also counts the format's own price basis.
- Part 2: does the creator perform the brand's message? If the brand's graphic is inserted with no creator performance, the item is display (SR-performs-message).
- Part 3: the unit and the terms.
- Part 4: the entry or redemption terms.
- Part 5: the parts of the events, each with its own terms.
- Part 6: which line carries the targeting, and how the segment is built.
- The attention claim: the source and the method. The claim is the seller's.
