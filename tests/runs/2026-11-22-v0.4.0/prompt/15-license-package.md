Route: prompt/PROMPT.md. A fresh subagent. Input given:

> License package: you get the right to use our game characters on your product packaging. Your logo appears on a billboard inside the game. You commit to a media campaign to promote the launch.

## Answer

Input type: an offer. I use the offer form. Every fact below is the seller's claim.

The deal name is "License package." The name does not decide the line. Each payment goes to its own line (S-6.1, SR-payments).

**1. What it is**

| # | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Right to use the game's characters on the brand's product packaging |
| 2 | The brand's logo on a billboard inside the game |
| 3 | The brand's commitment to a media campaign to promote the launch |

**2. Where each part goes**

| # | Line | Compare with | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Licensing | Royalty, guarantee, term, and territory | M-20 |
| 2 | No native line. See part 3. | The second sentence of the rule: a currency or a record | M-22, M-27 |
| 3 | The line of the media the brand gives. It is listed as part of the price. It is unplaced until the units are named. | Not yet known | M-23, S-6.3, SR-in-kind, S-6.6 |

Notes:
- Part 1: the brand pays for the right. The money does not go to the brand. So S-6.2 does not apply.
- Part 2: the license name does not move this part into Licensing. M-22 covers the brand's marks inside play under any deal name, including a license.
- Part 3: the offer gives no units. S-6.6 says the part is unplaced until the seller names the units.

**3. What has no native line**

Part 2 is a brand's presence inside play (M-27).
- Route stated: neither. The offer states no currency and no record.
- No comparison is stated. So no comparison meets the four requirements (R-1 to R-4).
- Result by the table "Three places": production plus delivery, on partnership terms.
- Any engagement count the seller gives for part 2 is media engagement. No comparison comes with it (C-3.2).
- If the billboard is a standard creative in a slot, it is display. Cite M-01. The offer does not say this. See part 4.

I give no verdict on whether the buy works.

**4. What to confirm**

- Part 1: the royalty, the guarantee, the term, and the territory. Which characters. Which products and packaging. Whether the seller approves the use.
- Part 2: is the logo a standard creative in a slot (M-01)? If yes, what is the unit, the price basis, and the currency? Who counts it?
- Part 2: if it is not a standard creative, does the order hold a currency or a record? Does a comparison meet R-1 to R-4?
- Part 2: does the seller count the price on a measure other than the format's own price basis? If so, N-9 applies.
- Part 2: any engagement claim. The source and the method. The claim is the seller's.
- Part 3: the media in the campaign. Their units, price bases, and amounts. Who buys them. Whether the media is given to the seller or to third parties.
- Part 3: is the media part of the price of the deal?
- The price of each part. If the offer sets one price for all three parts, ask for the price of each.
- Whether any money is paid to the brand. If so, that money is outside the test (S-6.2).
