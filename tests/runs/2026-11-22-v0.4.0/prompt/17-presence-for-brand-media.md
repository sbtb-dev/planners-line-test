Any attention claim would be compared inside the line of the part it describes. The offer makes none.

**3. What has no native line**

Part 1 is a brand's presence inside play (M-27).

- Route stated: neither. The offer states no currency. The offer states no record.
- Comparison: none is stated. So no comparison meets the four requirements (R-1 to R-4).
- Engagement counts: the offer gives none. If the seller gives any, label them media engagement (C-3.2).
- If the product is a standard creative in a slot, the first row (M-01) places it. The offer does not say so.
- Without a route, the item falls to production plus delivery, on partnership terms (M-27, M-25).

This is a report. It gives no verdict on whether the buy works.

**4. What to confirm**

- What the game shows: the product as a standard creative in a slot, or a branded item, skin, or world. Ask the seller.
- The unit and the price basis of part 1. Ask whether the seller counts any impression, and who counts it.
- Whether the seller offers a record in the order: the unit, the exposed population, a comparison that separates the brand from the venue, and a verifier.
- Whether the brand builds anything for part 1. A paid build is production (M-25).
- For part 2: the number of posts, the accounts, the format, the dates, and who writes the message.
- For part 3: the size, the placement, the print run, the term, and the approval of the brand's own artwork.
- Whether any money moves to the brand or from the brand. Money paid to the brand is outside the test (S-6.2).
- Whether the seller states a value for each part. If not, ask for the value of each part.
