**3. What has no native line**

The offer states no brand presence inside play. Route stated: neither a currency nor a record. No comparison is offered, so none meets the four requirements (R-1 to R-4). No engagement count is given.

**4. What to confirm**

- Which units the budget buys. Ask the seller to name each unit.
- The price basis and the currency of each unit.
- Whether the budget is one payment or several. Each payment goes to its own line (S-6.1).
- Whether the commitment can change, or whether the seller can refuse it, before the units are named.
- What "better placement" means. Ask for a written definition and the conditions.
- What "priority access" means. Ask what it gives, to whom, and for how long.
- Whether the seller gives the same terms to a buyer that does not commit. This is a claim to check.
- Whether the commitment has a fee, a bundle, a guarantee, or a date. The seller has not stated any.
- Whether the brand gives media or assets as part of the deal (S-6.3). If so, list them as part of the price.
