## F-2: does the proposal state a route?

No. The proposal states no currency and no record (N-3). **Not a boundary** for this part. The "gaming" label has no conditions. It states no unit, no exposed population, no comparison, and no verifier. Also, "all gaming offers go in it" lets the offer's label decide membership. A boundary sorts items before the buy, and the seller does not decide membership (N-4). Steps F-3 and F-4 are not reached.

What is missing: a currency (the same defined event, counted the same way by every seller, by a party that is not the host: C-3.1) or a record (the four requirements R-1 to R-4: C-3.2).

## What to confirm

- Whether the proposer wants one budget with one owner and one benchmark (section 4 of the rule). A mixed line of display, video, creator, and other units has no single benchmark.
- Which units the proposer means by "gaming offers."
- Whether any offer counts its price on a measure that is not its format's own price basis.
- For any presence inside play: which route the seller states (a currency, a record, or neither), and whether any comparison meets R-1 to R-4. Engagement counts are media engagement unless a comparison comes with them (C-3.2).
