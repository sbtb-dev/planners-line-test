**2. Where each part goes**

| # | Line | Compare with | Cite |

Each payment goes to its own line (S-6.1, SR-payments). The deal name "gaming campaign" does not decide the line.

"Expect strong results" is a claim from the seller. It has no unit and no comparison. It is not placed.

**3. What has no native line**

Nothing in this offer is a brand's presence inside play. In-game display is a standard creative in a slot (M-01). Route stated: not applicable. No engagement count is in the offer.

**4. What to confirm**

- One price covers six parts. Ask for the price of each part (S-6.1).
- Part 1: the unit, the price basis, and the currency. Are the impressions viewable? Who counts them?
- Part 2: the price basis. Is it completed views or another measure? Who counts them?
- Part 3: does the creator perform the brand's message? If the brand's graphic is inserted with no creator performance, it is display (SR-performs-message). Also confirm the unit and terms.
- Part 4: the unit and the terms.
- Part 5: the unit and the currency. Is it CTV or broadcast? Who counts it?
- Part 6: who runs the study, what it measures, and its method. The seller supplies it, so the results are the seller's claims.
- Whether the "strong results" claim has a source, a metric, and a method.
- The currency and the terms for the whole offer. The offer states none.
