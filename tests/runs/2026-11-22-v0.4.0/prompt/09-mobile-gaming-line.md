> We propose a mobile-gaming line. Mobile gaming has higher attention, so it needs its own benchmark.

## Answer

**Input type:** A proposed line. Use the proposed-line form.

**Result: Placed.** Step: F-1.

**Why**

- "Mobile gaming" is a venue and audience label. A line with this name holds units that already have lines (F-1). Place each unit in its own line.
- The proposal says mobile gaming has higher attention. Attention does not change the unit (N-2). It is a property of the delivery. It is compared inside the line of the unit.
- The reason given for a new benchmark is attention only. The proposal gives no new price basis. So N-9 does not apply. The proposal names no route (currency or record). Step F-2 is not reached.

**Where the units go**

The proposal names no units. These are the lines for formats sold as gaming. Use the one that matches each unit.

| Sold as | Line | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Display, overlays, app inventory | Display | M-01, M-02 |
| Video, rewarded video | Video | M-03 |
| Audio | Audio | M-04 |
| Playable ad | Display, video, or performance, by the price basis of the buy | M-06, SR-price-basis |
| Install offers, offerwalls | Performance | M-18 |
| A gaming audience segment on another line | Targeting on that line | M-24, SR-targeting |

**Exception**

A brand's presence inside play (branded worlds, integrations, skins, digital goods, branded games) has no native line (M-27). If the proposal includes it, go to F-2. The proposal states no route for it. For that part the result is Not a boundary at F-2. The missing item is a currency or a record.

**What to confirm**

- Which units the line holds (format and price basis for each).
- Whether any unit is priced on attention or time in place of its own price basis. If yes, N-9 applies and that unit is unplaced.
- Whether the proposal includes a brand's presence inside play.
- The source and method of the attention claim. The claim is the seller's. It is compared inside each line.

This result applies to the description given. It is not a verdict on any seller or buy.
