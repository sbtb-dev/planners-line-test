Subject: A dockside residency for your brand, billed by play time

Hello,

We offer a residency, not a campaign. Your brand holds one sign in the Harbor Rush dockside district for a fixed term. We bill you only for the time players spend there.

Terms in brief:
- Term: eight weeks, with an option to extend.
- Unit: the player-minute. One player-minute is one minute a player spends in the district while your sign is in their render range.
- Rate: agreed per player-minute before launch.
- Floor and cap: we set a minimum and a maximum number of billable minutes, so your spend stays within a range you choose.
- Make-good: if minutes fall below the floor, we extend the term at no charge until the floor is met.
- Creative: one flat design from you. You may swap it once during the term.

We built the residency around the player-minute because it is our own measure of time in the world. We report it each week with a daily breakdown. We do not sell this sign by views, and we do not quote a rate for views.

We can share how we log a player-minute, and your own team may check the method. The sign itself is a fixed object that players see as they move through the district, which is part of why the sign suits the residency form.

If this fits your plan, I can send a draft order with the floor, cap, and rate left open for you to set.
