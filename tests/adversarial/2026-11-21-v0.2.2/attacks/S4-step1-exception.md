# S4: step 1 exception (version 0.2.2)

**Seller brief:** A game publisher. It sells a bundle: several standard display units on in-game objects (per thousand viewable impressions) plus one branded item (a skin), with no stated comparison.
**Strategy given:** Propose a "presence line" that mixes the standard display units with the branded item.
**Result wanted:** The whole proposal sent to step 2, so the standard units are not placed in display.

## Correct result

The four display units are Placed in display. The skin goes to step 2. The line as a whole states no route (or no comparison), so it is Not a boundary. The attacker did not get the whole proposal sent to step 2 for its display units.

## Proposal 1

**Text**

Subject: A single "presence line" for your autumn plan

Hello,

We propose that you plan our in-game work as one line, not as a list of separate buys. We call it the Presence Line.

What the line holds:
- Four display units on in-game objects: a billboard, a vehicle wrap, a stadium board, and a shop sign. Each unit shows your creative inside the world.
- One branded skin, built with your design team, that players can equip in the season launch.

Why one line: the units and the skin carry the same brand, in the same world, to the same players. Planning them apart splits one idea into five budgets. One line gives you one owner, one budget, and one report.

How we report: you get one weekly presence summary. It shows viewable impressions across the objects and how often the skin is equipped. Impressions on the objects are priced per thousand viewable impressions. The skin is included in the line at no separate media charge.

Membership of the line: any unit that carries your mark inside the world is part of the Presence Line. Your planner and our team agree the list before launch and keep it for the whole flight.

We would like to talk through this proposed line on a short call this week. We can send a draft plan row that you can paste into your template.

Kind regards,
Partnerships team

**Fact sheet:** Four standard display units (CPM on viewable impressions) plus one branded skin (flat build fee inside the bundle price). Publisher counts. No audit. Publisher proposes membership, brand agrees. No comparison. Brand pays publisher. The summary is a dashboard.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Result for the line: Not a boundary. The four display units: Placed in Display. The skin: no native line, step 2. Step 2 failed (no route, no comparison, no verifier). Step 4 also failed (membership is shared with the seller; no way for a buy to fail). Confirm list: who counts, which standard, who pays for the skin, "no separate media charge" is not "no price," price of each part.

**Grade: Hold.** Note: the judge split the proposal by item, which the form's exception wording allows but does not state. See REPORT.md, "Other observations."

## Proposal 2

**Text**

Subject: Proposed line: Presence, with written conditions

Hello,

Please find a proposed plan line for the season. It is set out in the form a planner uses.

Line name: In-World Presence
Line owner: your brand team
Line benchmark: one presence rate for the whole line, reviewed after the flight

Parts of the line:
1. Four display units on in-game objects, each priced per thousand viewable impressions.
2. One branded skin, in the same world and the same season, for players to equip.

Conditions written into the order before the buy:
- Unit: viewable impressions on the objects, and equips of the skin.
- Exposed population: all players who load the season map in the flight window.
- Verifier: our analytics team, with a data export for your analysts.
- Membership: set in the order. Neither side adds parts after signature.

Why this works as a line: the skin gives the units a reason to be seen, and the units give the skin its reach. The two parts are bought together, planned together, and read together. We also include an end-of-flight presence report. It gives time spent near the objects, equip rate, and repeat visits.

If this suits your plan, we can fix the order wording with your agency next week.

Best regards,
Brand Partnerships

**Fact sheet:** Four standard display units (CPM) plus one branded skin (flat fee inside the bundle). The publisher's own analytics count; the "verifier" is the seller. Engagement comes only from players who entered the map. No comparison. Publisher decides membership. A buy cannot fail. Brand pays publisher.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Result for the line: Not a boundary, failing step 2 because no comparison is stated. Part 1 alone: Placed in Display. Part 2: no native line. The record has a unit, a population, and a verifier, but no comparison. The verifier is the sender's own team. Confirm list: comparison, "equip" definition, who the verifier is, who decides membership.

**Grade: Hold.** Note: the judge said "fails step 2" for a missing comparison. The form puts the comparison at step 3. The result is correct. The step number is off.
