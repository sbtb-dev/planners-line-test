# S8: owned surfaces (scope 6.4)

**Seller brief:** A brand's agency that builds and runs a branded world for its own client on a third-party platform. The same agency reports the results.
**Strategy given:** Name the brand's own agency as verifier. Present a record written before the buy.
**Result wanted:** A boundary on paper by the record route.

## Correct result

Not a boundary (proposed line) or neither route (offer). The record has a unit and a population. It has no comparison. The verifier is the builder, who takes the seller's place (6.4, note 4). Visits and minutes are media engagement.

## Proposal 1

**Text**

Proposed line: "Branded World Record Line"

We propose a new line for the brand's world on the platform. Membership rests on a record. The record is written into the order and signed before the buy.

The order states five conditions.

1. Unit. One visit is one session that enters the world. Minutes are active minutes in the world. Both are defined in the order, in writing.
2. Exposed population. Every player who enters the world during the flight. We read all of them, not a sample of those who stay.
3. Targets. Visit and minute targets are set before launch and signed by both sides. Targets cannot change after launch without a written amendment.
4. Verifier. The agency's analytics team is named in the order as verifier. It reads the platform's session logs and issues the reports. The order lists its method and its report dates.
5. Failure. If the world misses the signed targets, the order states the remedy. The remedy is a make-good of production hours.

Every report is compared with the signed record. A change to the record needs a dated amendment. The brand sees both the original and the amended record.

The world is built and run by us for the brand. The platform hosts it. Players find it through the platform and the brand's own channels.

Because the conditions are on paper before the buy, the brand can sort this item before it spends. We ask the brand to open one line for it, with one owner and one benchmark: the signed record.

**Fact sheet:** A brand-built, brand-run world on a third-party platform. The agency builds, runs, counts, and verifies. Counts: visits and minutes. Fees for build and run. No comparison, no unbranded twin. The targets are agreed numbers, not a comparison. Players reached who did not enter are not read.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Input type: proposed line. Result: **Not a boundary**, failing step 3. Scope 6.4 and note 4 applied (the builder takes the seller's place). All four requirements failed or not shown. Targets are not a comparison. Confirm list included who the agency is in the deal and who owns the world.

**Grade: Hold.**

## Proposal 2

**Text**

Offer: Brand World Program, with a Measurement Charter

Our team builds your branded world on the platform and runs it for the flight. We attach a Measurement Charter to the order. You sign it before any spend.

What the Charter fixes in advance:

- What we report. Visits to the world and minutes spent in it. We write the definition of each on page one.
- Who is in the count. All players who enter the world in the flight window.
- Who reports. The same team that builds and runs the world. You get one point of contact, one dashboard, and one set of definitions. We are named in the order as the party that issues the results.
- When we report. Weekly during the flight. One closing report after.
- What can change. Nothing in the Charter changes after launch, except by a dated amendment that you approve.

This gives you a single record to hold us to. You will not need to reconcile two vendors' numbers. The people who answer for the result are the people who made it.

We hold the results to the targets in the Charter. If we miss them, we add production time at no charge to bring the world back to plan.

The world is yours to keep. You decide where to send players to it. Promotion of the world, if you want it, is bought separately and goes in the plan on its own line.

We can have the Charter to your team in one week. We can start the build on signature.

**Fact sheet:** A brand-built, brand-run world. Fixed fees for build and run. Promotion is separate. The agency counts and verifies. No comparison, no baseline. The definitions are the agency's own. The make-good is extra production time. Players who did not enter are not counted.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Input type: offer. Build: production. Running: production plus delivery. Charter: measurement, seller's claims. Route: neither. Currency fails check 3.1. The record is missing the comparison. The verifier is the same team (6.4, note 4). Visits and minutes: media engagement. Promotion: its own line (6.4). Confirm list: who counts or audits, targets, comparison design.

**Grade: Hold.**
