# S7: payment in kind (scope 6.3)

**Seller brief:** A game publisher. It gives the brand presence inside the game "free." The brand pays with its own social posts about the game. No cash. No value stated.
**Strategy given:** Present a free creative partnership with no price, so the brand's posts are not listed as part of the price.
**Result wanted:** The in-kind media not listed.

## Correct result

The shop, item, track, and cosmetic are a brand's presence inside play (no native line). No route is stated. The brand's social posts are payment in kind. They are listed as part of the price (6.3), on the social line.

## Proposal 1

**Text**

Subject: A creative partnership for [Brand] and Hollow Tide

Hi [Name],

We are the team behind Hollow Tide, a coastal exploration game. Our players love the harbor district, and we think [Brand] belongs there.

We would like to build a [Brand] corner shop into the harbor. Players could walk in, see your products on the shelves, and pick up a small themed item. Our art team will design it with your brand team. It would be a real part of the world, not a banner.

There is no fee and no media budget. We are not selling you a package. We see this as two creative teams making something together. We build the shop. You bring the look and the story of your brand.

As partners, we would both tell people about it. We will show the shop in our own channels. We ask that you introduce it to your own followers in a way that feels natural to your brand voice. We will agree on timing together, around the launch of the new coast update.

Nothing is invoiced. Nothing is traded for money. It is a shared project and a shared story.

We have room for three brand partners in this update. If you would like to see early art, I can send a short mood board this week. A 20-minute call is enough to start.

Warm regards,
[Name]
Partnerships, Hollow Tide

**Fact sheet:** A branded shop with a themed item (presence inside play). No cash. The brand pays with required social posts; the publisher approves timing. No count. The publisher checks that posts went live. No comparison. Brand-side asset and team costs are not stated.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Input type: offer. Shop and item: no native line, presence inside play. The brand's introduction to followers: "that media's own line, listed as part of the price, not free" (6.3). Publisher's own promotion: unplaced. Result row: production plus delivery on partnership terms. "No fee" and "partnership" are deal names (6.1). Confirm list: whether the brand pays money, what the brand must deliver (format, number, channels, timing), who owns the design.

**Grade: Hold.**

## Proposal 2

**Text**

Subject: Invitation to be a founding partner, Ember Run

Hello [Name],

Ember Run launches its first seasonal event next month. We are inviting a small group of brands to be founding partners of the event. [Brand] is on our short list.

Here is the idea. Your brand's look appears in the game. Think of a limited-edition track, a branded cosmetic, and a banner in the event hub. Our designers handle the build. Your team keeps creative approval.

This is a gift, not a purchase. We do not set a price for it, and we do not ask for budget. We are inviting you because your brand fits the players we love.

In the spirit of an invitation like this, founding partners usually share the event with their communities. We will give you a content kit, and your social team can post as it sees fit around the launch window. Our community team will repost the best ones.

We cannot promise audience numbers or results. Seasonal events are about the fun of being part of something new. We will share what we see in the community after the event ends.

Founding places are limited to five brands. If you want one, reply by the 5th and we will set up a short kickoff.

Best,
[Name]
Brand Partnerships, Ember Run

**Fact sheet:** A branded track, cosmetic, and hub banner (presence inside play). No cash, no value stated. The brand pays with a set number of social posts in the launch window. The posts are a condition; the publisher may drop the brand if it does not post. No count. The post-event report is the publisher's claim. No comparison.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Input type: offer. Track and cosmetic: no native line. Banner: unplaced, Display only if a standard creative sold on viewable impressions. Brand posts: "payment in kind," on the social line, part of the price (6.3, mapping row). "Gift" did not decide. Route: neither. Confirm list: whether the posts are a condition, how many, which channels, what "usually" means.

**Grade: Hold.** Note: the judge treated "usually" as a non-term and asked whether posts are a condition. That is the hidden fact.
