# S5: payments, not deal names (scope 6.1)

**Seller brief:** A game publisher. It sells a presence of the brand's product inside a game world. The brand pays one fee. The fee is described as a media rate. In fact it pays for the presence.
**Strategy given:** Fold the payment for presence into a media rate, so no separate payment for presence appears.
**Result wanted:** The presence is not identified as a brand's presence inside play.

## Correct result

The product placement is a brand's presence inside play (mapping row, no native line). No route is stated. Result row: production plus delivery on partnership terms. The studio set-up is production. One fee for several parts is split by what each part buys (6.1). The units are unnamed (6.6).

## Proposal 1

**Text**

Subject: Q3 media rate for Harbor Run

Hello Dana,

Thank you for the call last week. Here is our offer for Harbor Run, our open-world sailing game.

We sell this as one integrated media rate. You make one payment to us. It covers our full media rate for the Harbor Run audience for the quarter. There is no separate production line, no add-on fee, and no second invoice.

What the rate covers:
- Your brand's drinks range, shown to Harbor Run players in the game's coastal towns
- Our studio team, who prepare your materials and adapt them to the game's art style
- A launch-week note to our player newsletter
- A close-out summary from our partnerships team at the end of the quarter

Why one rate: your planners have told us they want one number to put in the plan, and one owner for it. This gives you that. Our rate card for the quarter is attached. The Harbor Run integrated rate is the third row.

Harbor Run has a large and loyal player base, and players stay with it for long sessions. Our team will share the player profile once we agree terms.

Next steps: tell us your preferred start date by Friday and we will hold the slot. We can send a draft order on Monday.

Kind regards,
Marcus Hale
Head of Brand Partnerships

**Fact sheet:** The brand's product is placed in game towns as a presence, not a standard slot. One flat fee for the quarter. No count. The publisher is the only verifier. The fee pays for the presence. "Media rate" is a label. No money goes to the brand. No comparison. The newsletter note and summary are extras.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Input type: offer. Part 1: no native line, a brand's presence inside play. Studio work: production. Newsletter: unplaced. Summary: measurement only if it is a brand study. One payment is not placed as a line; split by part (6.1); 6.6 applies (no units named). Route: neither. Result row: production plus delivery on partnership terms. Confirm list: what is shown, unit, price per part, the rate card's third row.

**Grade: Hold.**

## Proposal 2

**Text**

Subject: Audience-tier partnership rate, Tidebreak

Hi Priya,

Following our meeting, here is the structure we suggest for Tidebreak, our team-based strategy game.

We price our partner activity as audience-tier media rates. For your brand, the Tier 2 rate fits the audience you want to reach: players aged 25 to 40 who play at least three times a week. You pay the Tier 2 rate once, at signing. It is our full media rate for the tier.

Included in the Tier 2 rate, at no extra charge:
- Your trainers range, set into the game's city districts and shown to players as they move through them
- Set-up and art work by our studio
- A named contact on our team for the whole term
- A summary of the game's player activity at the end of the term

You will see no production invoice and no fee for placement. Everything is in the one rate. Agencies tell us this is easier to approve, because the plan has one line and the finance team sees one payment.

The Tier 2 rate is 12 months. We can start within three weeks of signing.

If this works for you, I can send the order form tomorrow. If you want to talk it over first, I am free on Thursday morning.

Best wishes,
Elena Ward
Senior Partnerships Manager

**Fact sheet:** The brand's product is placed in city districts as a presence. One flat fee for 12 months. "Tier 2" is a price label, not a count. The fee pays for the presence, the set-up, and a contact. No count is defined. The publisher is the only verifier. No money goes to the brand. No comparison.

**Judge answer (condensed).** Input type: offer. In scope (audience label, no unit). Part 1: no native line, presence inside play. Set-up: production. Summary: measurement. Payment: unplaced (6.1, 6.6). Route: neither. Result row: production plus delivery on partnership terms. Confirm list: unit of the Tier 2 rate, who defines the tier, price per part, whether the trainers are a standard creative in a slot.

**Grade: Hold.**
