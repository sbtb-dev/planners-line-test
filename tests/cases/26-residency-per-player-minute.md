# A residency billed per player-minute

**Type:** Offer

## Input

> A residency: one sign in a dockside district for eight weeks. You supply one flat design. We bill per player-minute, one minute a player spends in the district while the sign is in render range. We do not quote a rate for views. We set a floor and a cap on billable minutes. If minutes fall below the floor, we extend the term at no charge. Our servers log the minutes. Your team can check the method.

## Expected result

- The sign is a flat creative on an in-world object. The format is display (mapping row: in-game display). It is not a brand's presence inside play.
- The price basis is a player-minute. It is not the display price basis. The unit is unplaced (notes 6, 7, and 9).
- The floor, the cap, the make-good, and the term are deal terms (note 2).
- The host counts the minutes. No route is stated: no currency, no record.
- Player-minutes are media engagement. No comparison comes with them.
- What to confirm: whether the creative is standard, and whether the seller also counts viewable impressions for the sign.
