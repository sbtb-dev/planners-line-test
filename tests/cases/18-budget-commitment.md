# A commitment of budget before units are named

**Type:** Offer

## Input

> Commit a budget to our gaming portfolio for the year. We will decide the units with you later. The commitment gets you better placement and priority access.

## Expected result

- Unplaced until the seller names the units (scope 6.6).
- Say what is missing: the units, the price basis, and the terms of each.
- Placement and priority access are claims by the seller. They are terms, compared within the line once a line exists.
