# Presence in a game in exchange for the brand's media

**Type:** Offer

## Input

> We will place your product in our game. In exchange, you post about the game on your social accounts and give us space on your product packaging. No fee is paid.

## Expected result

- Presence in the game: a brand's presence inside play. No native line. Route stated: neither. Report only.
- The brand's media (social posts, packaging space) is listed as part of the price (scope 6.3; second rule: payment in kind). It is not free.
- Each part of the brand's media goes to its own line. Confirm the units of each.
