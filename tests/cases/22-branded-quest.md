# A branded quest that rewards players only in the branded version

**Type:** Proposed line

## Input

> We propose a branded quest line. Players who finish the branded quest get an in-game reward. The unbranded version has no reward. We compare completion in the two versions. The branded version completes more.

## Expected result

**Not a boundary.** The comparison fails requirement 1 of the comparison check (step 3).
- The reward appears only where the brand appears. A reward given only where the brand appears is a second change. The venue is not held constant.
- The difference cannot be assigned to the brand alone.
