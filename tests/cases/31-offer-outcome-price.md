# A sponsored listing billed per qualified lead

**Type:** Offer

## Input

> Our sponsored listing sits at the top of our directory results. We bill per qualified lead. A lead is a visitor who submits a quote request to the advertiser. The advertiser's own sales system counts the leads. We do not offer a comparison.

## Expected result

- The sponsored listing: performance, by the price basis of the buy (note 9 exception, N-5, N-7; mapping row M-18). A qualified lead is an outcome on the buyer's side, counted by a party that is not the host. It is the performance price basis, whatever the format.
- Do not mark the part "unplaced" because of its price basis (N-9 does not apply).
- Compare on cost per action against other performance units priced on the same defined event.
- The count supports a price for the outcome. It does not show that the brand caused the leads (N-5). No comparison is required for this price basis (C-3.2).
- What to confirm: the format of the listing, the exact definition of a qualified lead, and whether the advertiser can audit the count. No price, no recommendation.
