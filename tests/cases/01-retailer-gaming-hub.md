# A retailer's gaming hub

**Type:** Offer

## Input

> A retailer's gaming hub. We sell onsite placements on the hub, creator content, social amplification, sweepstakes, in-store events, and targeting on gamer profiles. One package price. Gamers pay high attention on our hub.

## Expected result

- Onsite placements: retail media.
- Creator content: creator (confirm that the creator performs the message).
- Social amplification: social.
- Sweepstakes: promotions.
- In-store events: experiential, by parts.
- Targeting on gamer profiles: not a unit. It is targeting on the line that carries it.
- The attention claim is the seller's claim. It is compared inside each line.
- Confirm the price of each part. No price, no recommendation.
