# An answer unit billed per follow-up, with the runner-up in the control

**Type:** Proposed line

## Input

> We sell an AI assistant. We propose an "answer line." Our standard units sit below answers and are sold per thousand impressions. The new unit is billed per qualified follow-up: within three turns of the unit, the user asks a follow-up in the advertiser's product category. We log the follow-ups. The order states these terms before the flight. A random 10% of eligible conversations are held out. In the held-out group, the user sees a unit of the same format, size, and position. The advertiser that placed second in the auction fills it. The reading covers all eligible conversations in both groups. Only the difference is the brand's. An independent firm computes the report and signs it. A make-good applies if there is no significant lift.

## Expected result

**A boundary on paper** (F-5), by the record route.
- The price basis of the new unit is a follow-up, counted by the seller on its own surface. It differs from the price basis of the standard units (note 9, N-9). The record route is stated.
- R-1 to R-4 are met. Only the brand changes. The slot, the unit, and the position stay the same.
- In practice: the verifier's records show a difference from the control.

**Note for the reader of the answer:** an answer that demands an unbranded twin is too strict. An unbranded twin is one design that meets R-1. Another design can also meet it (C-3.2, the paragraph on other designs).
