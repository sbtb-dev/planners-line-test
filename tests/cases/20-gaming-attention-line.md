# A gaming attention line at a premium

**Type:** Proposed line

## Input

> We propose a gaming attention line. It holds display, video, and audio units in games. Each unit has an attention score from an accredited attention vendor. A higher score earns a premium.

## Expected result

**Placed.** The units are display, video, and audio. They keep their lines (step 1).
- Attention is a property of the delivery. It is compared within the line (note 2).
- A higher score earns a higher price within the line. It does not make a new unit (notes 6 and 7).
