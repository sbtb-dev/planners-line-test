# An answer unit billed per follow-up, with a holdout that removes the slot

**Type:** Proposed line

## Input

> We sell an AI assistant. We propose an "answer line." Our standard units sit below answers and are sold per thousand impressions. The new unit is billed per qualified follow-up: within three turns of the unit, the user asks a follow-up in the advertiser's product category. We log the follow-ups. The order states these terms before the flight. A random 10% of eligible conversations are held out. In the held-out group the slot is suppressed, and the answer shows no unit. The reading covers all eligible conversations in both groups. Only the difference is the brand's. An independent firm computes the report and signs it. A make-good applies if there is no significant lift.

## Expected result

**Not a boundary** (F-3).
- The price basis of the new unit is a follow-up, counted by the seller on its own surface. It is not an outcome on the buyer's side. It differs from the price basis of the standard units (note 9, N-9). The first sentence does not place it, so the form goes to step 2.
- The record route is stated. R-2, R-3, and R-4 are met, and a verifier is named.
- R-1 fails. The seller produces the event on its own surface and counts it, so a comparison is needed (C-3.2). The control removes the slot. The slot is part of the seller's surface. The venue is not held constant.
- The follow-ups are media engagement.

**Note for the reader of the answer:** an answer that passes R-1 because the holdout is randomized has misread R-1. Random choice decides who is in each group. R-1 asks what the two groups see.
