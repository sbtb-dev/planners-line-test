# Input: Overwolf offer (summary)

Every fact is the seller's claim.

Overwolf sells three products. Standard Media: IAB display and video in high-attention placements, served in Overwolf's own overlay and companion and tracker apps on top of games such as Fortnite and Apex Legends. Custom Media: takeovers and skins. Custom Activations: challenges, tournaments, and achievement rewards built around the brand. Ads can be triggered by in-game moments, for example a message on screen when a player scores a kill. "Rewarded-moments triggered ads" give players in-game rewards for achievements; the seller does not say what the rewards are or who grants them. Targeting uses session length, time of day, hardware, and games played. One contract covers more than 5,000 games. Case: Wendy's and Takis ran an opt-in in-game challenge in Fortnite, Marvel Rivals, and R.E.P.O.; players unlocked rewards; the seller reports 48K hours of challenge gameplay, purchase intent, and consideration, with no baseline and no named provider. Case: Epic Games bought video and display units in Overwolf apps to acquire new Fortnite players; Overwolf served the impressions, matched players in its platform, detected Fortnite installs, and attributed 160,864 installs to impressions. No holdout is described. The price basis is not stated.
