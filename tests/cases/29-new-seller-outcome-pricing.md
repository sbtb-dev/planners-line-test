# A new seller bills per booked appointment

**Type:** Proposed line

## Input

> We are a new seller. We propose a line for our sponsored unit. We bill per booked appointment. The advertiser's own booking system counts the appointments. We offer no comparison.

## Expected result

**Placed** (F-1) in performance.
- The price basis is an outcome on the buyer's side. The line compares units on cost per action (mapping row M-18, by its price basis; notes 5 and 7, N-5 and N-7).
- A party that is not the host counts the outcome. No comparison is required for this price basis (C-3.2). The count does not show that the brand caused the appointments.

**Note for the reader of the answer:** an answer that asks for an unbranded twin or a holdout treats the comparison check as a gate for every unit. It is not. It applies when the seller produces the event on its own surface and counts it.
