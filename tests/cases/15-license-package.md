# A license package with three parts

**Type:** Offer

## Input

> License package: you get the right to use our game characters on your product packaging. Your logo appears on a billboard inside the game. You commit to a media campaign to promote the launch.

## Expected result

- Characters on packaging: licensing. Compare on royalty, guarantee, term, and territory.
- Logo inside the game: a brand's presence inside play. No native line. Route stated: neither. Report only. Production plus delivery, on partnership terms.
- Media commitment: each unit goes to its own line, once the units are named. Until then, unplaced (scope 6.6).
- Cite "payments, not deal names" (second rule; rule, section 6.1). The name "license package" does not place the parts.
