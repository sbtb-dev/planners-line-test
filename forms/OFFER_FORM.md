# Offer form

Use this form for any item or market move that is sold on a label. Fill it in from the seller's own material. That material is a claim, and the answer says so.

| Part | What it says |
|---|---|
| **1. What it is** | The parts in the offer, item by item |
| **2. Where each part goes** | Its line and what to compare it with. Cite the mapping row or the reading note. Any attention claim is compared in that line. |
| **3. What has no native line** | Which route the offer states: a currency, a record, or neither. Any engagement count is labeled media engagement unless a comparison comes with it. |
| **4. What to confirm** | Missing units, currencies, and terms, and claims to check with the seller |

Rules for the form:

- Cite a mapping row or a reading note for every placement. If none applies, write "unplaced" and say what information is missing.
- Do not supply a unit, currency, or term that the seller did not state. List it under part 4.
- Give no price, no recommendation, and no ranking of sellers.

## Filled example

The offer below is generic. It is written in a seller's voice, and every fact in it is the seller's claim.

> **Offer (seller's words, summarized):** A retailer's gaming hub. It sells onsite placements on the hub pages, creator content, social amplification, sweepstakes, in-store events, and targeting on gamer profiles.

**1. What it is**

| # | Part |
|---|---|
| 1 | Onsite placements on the hub |
| 2 | Creator content |
| 3 | Social amplification |
| 4 | Sweepstakes |
| 5 | In-store events |
| 6 | Targeting on gamer profiles |

**2. Where each part goes**

| # | Line | Compare with | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retail media | That retailer's other units, and other retailers' units | Mapping row: retailer placements and hubs |
| 2 | Creator | The creator line's units | Mapping row: creator package (second rule: performs-the-message) |
| 3 | Social | The social line's units | Mapping row: paid social inside a package |
| 4 | Promotions | Cost per entry or redemption | Mapping row: sweepstakes, codes, sampling |
| 5 | Experiential, by parts | Each part separately | Mapping row: launch events, tournaments, play spaces |
| 6 | Not a unit. It is targeting on the line that carries it. | That line's other segments, on the same units | Mapping row: a gaming audience segment sold on another line (second rule: targeting is not a unit) |

Any attention claim in the offer is compared inside the line of the part it describes.

**3. What has no native line**

Nothing in this offer is a brand's presence inside play. Route stated: not applicable.

**4. What to confirm**

- The unit and the currency for part 1 (impressions? viewable? by whom counted?).
- Whether the creator performs the brand's message in part 2.
- The unit and the terms for part 3.
- The entry or redemption terms for part 4.
- The parts of part 5, each with its own terms.
- Which line carries the targeting in part 6, and how the segment is built.
- Whether the offer sets one price for all six parts. If so, ask for the price of each part.
- The source and method of any attention claim. The claim is the seller's.

*A model's or a planner's answer is not a record. Results vary.*
